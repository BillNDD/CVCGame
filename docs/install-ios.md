# Install Word Quest on an iPad or an iPhone

This document tells you how to put the app on an iOS home screen. The steps use Safari.
This document obeys ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.

## What you need

- An iPad with iPadOS 15.4 or later, or an iPhone with iOS 15.4 or later.
- Safari. Other browsers on iOS cannot add an app to the home screen.
- The app address. The default address is `https://billndd.github.io/CVCFame/`.

## Install the app

1. Open Safari.
2. Go to the app address.
3. Wait until the home screen of the game shows the "Begin Session" control.
4. Tap the share control. The control shows a square with an arrow that points up.
5. Scroll down in the share list.
6. Tap "Add to Home Screen".
7. Keep the name "Word Quest".
8. Tap "Add" at the top right.

The Word Quest symbol appears on the home screen. The app opens in its own window, without the Safari controls.

## Use the app offline

1. Open the app one time with a network connection.
2. The app then operates without a network connection.

The app stores all progress on the device. The app makes no network calls after the first load.

## Give microphone permission

1. Start a session in microphone mode.
2. Tap "Start Recording".
3. iOS asks for microphone permission one time. Tap "Allow".

If you tap "Don't Allow", the app changes to adult mode. In adult mode, you give the results with the hold controls.

## Remove the app

1. Hold the Word Quest symbol on the home screen.
2. Tap "Remove App".
3. Tap "Delete App".

iOS then removes the saved progress after a time. Make a backup file first in the "Grown-ups corner".
